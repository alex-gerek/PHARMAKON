1. THE PROBLEM: INSTRUMENT BLEED & NEURAL BLOAT
Modern stem demixing has predominantly migrated to heavy deep learning architectures (HTDemucs, MDX23, BS-Roformer). While they produce impressive results in offline studio environments, they introduce severe production friction:
- Downloading 2 to 6 Gigabytes of PyTorch weights.
- Prohibitive GPU requirements (CUDA or Apple Silicon).
- Pervasive phase artifacts (diffusion warble, smudged drum transients).

The main headache for producers working with generative audio (Suno v3.5/v4, Udio 1.5) is acoustic bleed: rhythm and lead guitars aggressively leak into the vocal stem because distorted electric guitar overtones share the exact same frequency zone as human vocal formants: 300 - 3800 Hz.

Does clean stem separation truly demand a multi-gigabyte server cluster?
No. By leveraging classical time-frequency geometric distribution and inter-channel statistical coherence, stem demixing executes via pure deterministic mathematics in ~3 seconds on a standard laptop CPU.

2. THE 4-STEM DSP PIPELINE
In PHARMAKON 2.0, stem separation operates as a zero-phase pipeline across the STFT complex plane:

Stage 1. Infrasonic & Sub-Bass Isolation (< 120 Hz):
Steep 4th-order Butterworth crossover. All acoustic energy below 120 Hz is consolidated into a pure True-Mono stream: M = 0.5 * (L + R), guaranteeing 100% mono punch and zero subwoofer phase cancellation.

Stage 2. Harmonic-Percussive Source Separation (Ono 2D Median Filter):
Decomposes spectrogram magnitudes into:
- Continuous horizontal harmonic formants (vocal sustains, guitars, chords).
- Broadband vertical transient spikes (snare attack, kick punch, cymbal hits).

Stage 3. Wiener Mask & Phantom Center Vocal Dome (300 - 3800 Hz):
Isolates the phantom stereo center and applies a smooth Wiener formant dome covering 300 to 3800 Hz. Off-axis guitars and stereo width instruments are cleanly filtered out.

Stage 4. The Residual Stem (Other):
Strict phase-null subtraction:
Other = Full_Mix - (Bass + Drums + Vocals)
Summing all 4 stems reconstructs the master file with exact floating-point precision to 0.0 dB without phase distortion.
